IPv4 vs IPv6 Checker

This tool checks which internet protocol your connection uses, by asking the public ipify service for your address over IPv4 and over IPv6.

Click the button to check.

How it works: api.ipify.org answers only over IPv4, and api64.ipify.org answers over IPv6 when your connection supports it. If the IPv6 check fails, your connection is IPv4-only — which is normal and fine.
