What's My IP Address?

Your public address, your approximate location, and your provider — explained in plain English.

Your IP address

This tool fetches your public IP address using the public ipify API (api.ipify.org), then looks up its approximate location and provider using the public ipapi.co API. The location is approximate — accurate to the city level at best — and nothing about you is stored by this site.

Click the button to see your public IP address, location, and ISP.
